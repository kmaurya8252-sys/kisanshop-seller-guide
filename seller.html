KisanShop Seller Guide

Your step-by-step guide to becoming a KisanShop seller

Welcome, Seller!

Want to sell your agricultural products directly to farmers across India? KisanShop makes it easy!

STEP 1

Seller Registration

To become a KisanShop seller, first contact the KisanShop team through WhatsApp and share the following documents and business details.

Required Documents & Details

  • PAN Card
  • Business Registration Certificate
  • GST Certificate
  • Bank Passbook or Cancelled Cheque
  • Business Name & Address
  • Shop PIN Code
  • Mobile Number & Email ID
Note: Aadhaar Card and product list are not required for this registration process. Products can be added later through the Seller Panel.
STEP 2

Get Your Seller Account

After receiving your documents and business details, the KisanShop team will create your seller account.

Once your account is created, you will receive your Seller ID / login credentials and access to the KisanShop Seller Panel.

STEP 3

Login to Seller Panel

Open the KisanShop Seller Portal using the link provided by the KisanShop team.

Enter your registered Email ID and Password, then click on Sign In.

STEP 4

Explore Your Seller Dashboard

After successful login, you will reach the Seller Dashboard. Here you can monitor your business activities in one place.

Your Dashboard Includes:

  • Live Products
  • Pending Payout
  • Delivered Orders
  • In-Transit Orders
  • Orders Snapshot
  • Stock Alerts
  • Top-Selling Products
  • Sales by State
You can manage and track your products, orders, payments and sales directly from your dashboard.
